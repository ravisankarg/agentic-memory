Research method

How this atlas handles claims

  • Primary papers, official repositories, and official product documentation are preferred over secondary summaries.
  • Reported benchmark results are attributed to the reporting paper; they are not assumed independently reproduced.
  • “State of the art” is limited to a benchmark/setup and treated as time-sensitive.
  • Public datasets, judge models, and repository contents can drift. The benchmark guide records known version mismatches.
  • Proposed schemas and architecture recommendations are labeled as synthesis, not standards.
Important caveat

Cross-paper memory scores are rarely apples-to-apples. Different writers, embedding models, readers, prompts, context budgets, judges, and dataset releases can move the result. Re-run candidate systems inside one pinned harness before choosing a design.
